# Thursday 19 November 2026 — in one image

A front-page infographic summing up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026:

- **Trump, Xi and Putin hold first-ever three-way summit** — Xi hosts Trump and Putin in Shenzhen at APEC; nuclear arms and the Iran war top the agenda, no deal expected with New START lapsed
- **Fed minutes point to more rate hikes** — nearly all officials expect further rises; markets price +25bp in December and two more by summer 2027
- **World court delivers Rohingya verdict** — the ICJ hands down its judgment in The Gambia v. Myanmar, a seven-year case over the 2017 crackdown
- **GTA VI rolls out around the world** — after 13 years, Rockstar's sequel launches at midnight local time; 99 on Metacritic, pre-orders top $1.5bn
- **Crimean Bridge hit a third time** — a fresh strike halts traffic on the Kerch Bridge, days after a road span collapsed into the strait
- **US accounts for $12bn of Venezuelan oil money** — the State Department publishes a 53-page report on Venezuelan oil and mineral revenue

The image was drawn from scratch in this session with `draw_entry.py`
(Python + PIL, Liberation fonts, no external images) and visually checked
for legibility and overflow. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only)
copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or `/output/entry.png`).
The image is a 1024×1024 PNG, 142,821 bytes — inside the 1 MiB / 1024×1024 limits.
